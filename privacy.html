Privacy Policy

Missouri Siding is an independent residential siding referral resource. This website has no contact form, account registration, or installed advertising analytics tracker.

Website visits

The hosting provider and external font, icon, and website component services may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and requested files when pages load.

Telephone inquiries

Calls may connect you with an independent service provider or referral partner. Information you choose to share may be used to respond to your inquiry. The answering party’s privacy practices apply. Ask that party about its identity, information handling, and any call recording before sharing sensitive information.

Your choices

You may choose not to call or share personal information. Direct questions about records or data requests to the party that received your information.
